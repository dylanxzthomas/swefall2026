# Wren

Wren is a personal voice assistant for managing work and social commitments. It schedules short conversations with you, prepares you for meetings, remembers what you tell it, and follows up on unfinished tasks.

- **Talk anytime:** Hold the main microphone button to speak, or type. Wren replies aloud and keeps a transcript.
- **Scheduled check-ins:** Morning planning, meeting preparation, evening debriefs, and reminders. Wren notifies you when it is time to talk.
- **Meeting and people briefs:** Review the agenda, who is attending, past conversations, and promises still open between you.
- **Personal memory:** Store preferences, relationships, facts with sources, and commitments. Review and edit what Wren remembers.
- **Background preparation:** Periodically review upcoming events and saved context while you are away. Prepare briefings and flag overdue promises or patterns worth discussing.
- **Social follow-up:** Remember calls you owe, details about friends, and things to bring up when you next see someone.
- **Task actions:** Create local calendar events, set reminders, track promises, and prepare email drafts for you to send.

The home screen centers on the conversation button. Schedule, People, Memory, and Settings hold the supporting details.

The MVP runs locally on a Mac with a browser interface. It reads calendar feeds and uses external AI and voice APIs. Model-backed features require API keys; the demo uses placeholder replies. Google Calendar writes and email inbox access are outside the current scope.

Briefings should be short, specific, and grounded in saved information. Wren should distinguish facts from guesses and stay quiet when it has nothing useful to add.
